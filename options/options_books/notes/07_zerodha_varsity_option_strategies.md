### Bullish Strategies
- **Ch 2: Bull Call Spread** — Buy lower strike call + sell higher strike call
- **Ch 3: Bull Put Spread** — Sell higher strike put + buy lower strike put (credit spread)
- **Ch 4: Call Ratio Back Spread** — Sell 1 ITM call + buy 2 OTM calls
- **Ch 5: Bear Call Ladder** — Sell 1 ITM call + buy 1 ATM call + buy 1 OTM call

### Bearish Strategies
- **Ch 7: Bear Put Spread** — Buy higher strike put + sell lower strike put
- **Ch 8: Bear Call Spread** — Sell lower strike call + buy higher strike call (credit spread)
- **Ch 9: Put Ratio Back Spread** — Sell 1 ITM put + buy 2 OTM puts

### Neutral / Volatility Strategies
- **Ch 10: Long Straddle** — Buy ATM call + buy ATM put (same strike, same expiry)
- **Ch 11: Short Straddle** — Sell ATM call + sell ATM put
- **Ch 12: Long & Short Strangle** — OTM call + OTM put (both buy or both sell)

### Analytical Tools
- **Ch 13: Max Pain & PCR Ratio** — Open interest analysis, put-call ratio for Nifty/Bank Nifty

### Range-bound
- **Ch 14: Iron Condor** — Bull put spread + bear call spread combined
