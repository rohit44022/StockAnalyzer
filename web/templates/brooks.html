How This Works
This scanner reads every stock's daily bars exactly the way Al Brooks describes in his three books. It classifies each bar (trend bar, doji, signal bar, climax), determines the always-in direction, detects spikes, channels, two-legged moves, and finds H1/H2/L1/L2 pullback entries, wedge/three-push reversals, failed breakouts, and second entries.

Stocks qualify for the Top 5 only if they pass Brooks' trader's equation (expected gain > expected loss), are trading with the always-in direction, score in the engine's high confidence band (70+), and offer at least 1.5x reward-to-risk. When nothing qualifies, the list is empty — that is the honest answer, not a failure.

Every pick shows exactly why it was selected: the setup type, trend phase, buying/selling pressure, active patterns, entry/stop/target levels, and a plain-English explanation of the trade.
