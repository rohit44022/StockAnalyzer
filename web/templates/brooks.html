How to trade with this scanner

  1. Click "Find Today's Trades" — it scans the entire stock universe and ranks the best swing BUY opportunities.
  2. Read the trade card — each card shows you the exact entry price, stop loss, and target. These are not guesses — they come from price structure (swing highs, swing lows, measured moves).
  3. Check the "Why This Trade" box — it explains in plain English what the price is doing and why the setup qualifies.
  4. Place the trade — buy at or near the Entry price, set a stop loss order at the Stop price. Hold for up to 30 trading days or until target/stop is hit.
  5. Position size — risk no more than 2% of your capital on any single trade. If entry is ₹100 and stop is ₹95, your risk is ₹5/share. With ₹10L capital, risk ₹20K, so buy 4,000 shares max.
